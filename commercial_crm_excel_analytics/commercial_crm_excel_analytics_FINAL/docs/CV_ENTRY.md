# CV Entry

**Commercial & CRM Operations Analysis | Excel**

- Built a formula-driven CRM analytics workbook covering 1,800 leads, using XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF/IFERROR, Excel Tables, data validation, conditional formatting, and management charts.
- Developed an executive dashboard for funnel conversion, channel efficiency, rep target attainment, open pipeline, weighted pipeline, stale opportunities, and monthly revenue performance.
- Identified a 54.4% Negotiation-to-Won drop-off, 358 stale open leads, and material channel-efficiency differences, translating findings into management actions.
