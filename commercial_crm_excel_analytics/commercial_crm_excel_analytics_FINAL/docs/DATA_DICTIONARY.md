# Data Dictionary

The full field dictionary is also included inside the workbook.

## Raw CRM fields

- Lead_ID — unique lead key
- Created_Date — lead creation date
- Owner — sales owner
- Region — sales region
- Raw_Source — unstandardized lead source
- Product_Code — product identifier
- Industry — customer industry
- Company_Size — SMB / Mid-Market / Enterprise
- Deal_Stage — current/final stage
- Last_Stage_Date — date of latest stage
- Close_Date — close date for won/lost deals
- Expected_Value_USD — expected deal value
- Discount_Pct — discount applied
- Stage_Probability — sales-stage probability
- Lost_Reason — reason for closed-lost deals
- Funnel_Level — furthest funnel stage reached
