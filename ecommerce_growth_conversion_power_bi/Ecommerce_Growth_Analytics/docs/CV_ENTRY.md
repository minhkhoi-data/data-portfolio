# CV Entry

**E-commerce Growth & Conversion Analytics | Power BI**

- Built a Power BI e-commerce analytics project using a star-schema semantic model with session, order, and order-item fact tables, reusable dimensions, Power Query transformations, and 30+ DAX measures.
- Analyzed 75,237 sessions and 4,737 transactions across funnel, channel, device, customer, geography, and product dimensions; identified product-view-to-cart as the largest funnel loss and a material mobile-vs-desktop conversion gap.
