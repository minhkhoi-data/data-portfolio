# Business Brief

## Scenario

An e-commerce growth team has traffic, transaction, product, and customer data but no single decision layer. Marketing reports traffic, merchandising reports product sales, and management sees revenue without knowing where conversion is being lost.

## Objective

Build a Power BI report that connects traffic quality, funnel progression, revenue, and product performance.

## Primary stakeholders

- Growth / Performance Marketing
- E-commerce Manager
- CRM / Lifecycle Marketing
- Merchandising
- Commercial / BI

## Questions

1. How much traffic and revenue are we generating?
2. What is the session conversion rate?
3. At which funnel step is the largest loss?
4. Which channels produce the strongest conversion and revenue per session?
5. Is mobile traffic monetizing as effectively as desktop?
6. Are returning users converting better than new users?
7. Which products and categories contribute most revenue?
8. Which countries or regions show strong commercial performance?

## Decision principles

The dashboard should avoid treating traffic volume as success by itself.

A channel should be judged using several metrics together:
- Sessions
- Conversion rate
- Revenue
- Revenue per session
- AOV

A product should be judged using:
- Revenue
- Units
- Orders
- Average selling price

A funnel stage should be judged using:
- Session count
- Step conversion
- Drop-off

## Non-goals

This project does not attempt attribution modelling, causal incrementality, media-mix modelling, or profit optimization because the demo dataset does not include complete cost, margin, or experiment data.
