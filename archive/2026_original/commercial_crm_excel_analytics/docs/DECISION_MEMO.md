# Evidence and proposed reviews

Organic search accounts for 2,296 sampled MQLs and 271 observed wins; paid search has 1,586 and 195. These are source-associated volumes, not causal channel effects. Comparison needs equal follow-up, acquisition spend, margins and consistent attribution before budget decisions.

The published samples record 842 wins among 8,000 leads. The other 7,158 have no won record, not a verified Lost/Open classification. The immediate instrumentation request is outcome/status and stage-entry history for non-won records, plus explicit sampling/extraction cutoffs.

One chronology anomaly is kept visible. Valid observed wins have a median calendar-day interval of 14 and a mean of approximately 48.5 days; the difference reflects a long right tail. With the explicit 30-day review threshold, 287 observed wins are slow. Reviewing that won-only group does not establish why other leads did not convert.

Only 380/842 won sellers match the independent commerce item sample; 376 have eligible Delivered item activity. The eligible item-price sum is 664,858.00 recorded units. Reconcile seller IDs, sample coverage and follow-up before comparing value across channels or representatives. No match must not become fabricated zero seller sales.

Preserve the difference between 60 missing origins and 1,099 explicit `unknown` origins. Review capture completeness and definitions with Marketing Ops. The source seller-declared monthly-revenue field has 797 zero entries, whose meaning is undocumented; it is not a trustworthy substitute for CRM won-deal value.

Action_List contains proposed owner roles, live baseline evidence, review windows and success measures. No test has been deployed, no contacts are authorized by this dataset, and no improvement is reported as measured.
